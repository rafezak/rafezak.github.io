Novolex: sales on the map

An interactive map that plots Novolex's sales and margin figures per distribution centre, so the sales team can see at a glance where the revenue and the margin are, and where they aren't.

All figures in the screenshots on this page are dummy data. Customer data, figures, and other business information are fictional. Novolex bears no responsibility for these representations.

Role
Design, data processing & front-end
Period
2025
Stack
JavaScript, Leaflet, CARTO
Context
Commissioned by Novolex

The brief

Novolex had its sales data neatly in order: in spreadsheets. Rows per order, columns per month, everything checked out. What was missing was the overview: a table tells you how much was sold, but not so easily where. And that's exactly what a sales team needs to decide where to spend its time.

The brief: make the geographic spread of revenue readable at a glance, without anyone first having to build a pivot table.

What I built

A web app internally called Sales · Margin Map. Novolex's distribution centres sit as points on a Leaflet map, and the same dataset can be viewed and filtered in several ways.

The map and its views

  • All DCs across Europe on one map, with a choice of three basemaps: light, dark, and OSM.
  • Four views of the same figures: bubbles (size = revenue), bars (margin vs. the previous period), heatmap, and a sortable table.
  • In the bar view, each DC gets a 3D bar: grey is the previous period, the coloured top is green for growth and red for decline, the height is the margin in euros and the width is the volume.

Filtering and zooming

  • Switching between product lines: Wrappers, Liners, or both.
  • Filtering to growers only or decliners only, plus a slider for the minimum margin (%) to hide weak DCs.
  • A search field to jump straight to a DC, and one Reset button that puts everything back.
  • A KPI bar at the top with revenue, margin €, margin %, cost, volume and lowest margin, that recalculates with whatever filters are active.

Detail per DC

  • Clicking a DC opens a side panel with a margin donut, revenue and volume, the cost-to-margin ratio, revenue by weekday (current vs. previous period), the period history (P1–P3), and the linked customers and items.
  • A compact hover popup with the key figures and the change in margin percentage points (pp) and in revenue.
  • A "Decliners / Risers" list at the bottom left with the biggest margin shifts, including country and the margin transition (for example 28.7% → 24.2%).

Getting data in

  • With "Upload Excel", Novolex loads its own export and the map recalculates everything, with no separate step through a BI tool.
  • The raw export is cleaned up and matched to coordinates before the map displays it.

What I learned

Data visualization is mostly an exercise in leaving things out. The first version of the map tried to show everything at once and became unreadable as a result. Using visual channels (colour, size, height, width) and offering multiple views is what actually made the data accessible, so you can see at a glance where the revenue sits, and where the pressure points in the business are.
