About me

I'm Raf Quaden (Maastricht), currently in my fourth year of HBO-ICT at Zuyd University of Applied Sciences. What draws me to this field is the moment something abstract becomes playable: an exercise schedule that turns into a game, a choice you only really understand once you've made it yourself.

That's also where I want to take my graduation internship. I'm looking for a place where serious gaming, gamification, or interaction design solves a real problem — where someone asks whether it actually works, not just whether it's finished.

What I work with: C#, TypeScript, React, Unity, Unreal Engine, Git, Figma. Alongside that, design and animation, mainly in SVG.
