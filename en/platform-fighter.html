Another Platform Fighter

A lightweight platform fighter for mobile. A study in combat that has weight, on a screen with no buttons.

Role
Everything: design, code, animation
Period
2025 – 2026
Stack
Unity, C#
Playable
on itch.io

What it's about

A platform fighter lives or dies by how a hit feels. Playing that same fight on a phone makes that harder: no buttons with resistance, no precision, and a thumb covering half the screen. So the question driving this project is: how do you give an attack weight when the player feels nothing under their fingers?

The answer isn't in any single thing but in the stacking of small effects: timing, delay at the moment of impact, how far a character flies back, how much control you briefly lose. Exactly the kind of system you only get right by playing and tweaking.

What I built

  • The combat system in Unity and C#, including hit detection, knockback and recovery frames.
  • Controls that work on a touchscreen, without giving up on precision entirely.
  • Character animation, tuned to how the attacks feel.
  • The whole thing kept light enough to run smoothly on ordinary phones.

What I learned

Making a fighting game feel good comes down to a lot of small things: timing, animation, hit feedback, recovery frames, sound, visual effects, hitstop, camera-shake. Everything contributes to how an attack feels. It's a system you can only get right through testing and critical feedback. On top of that, a mobile layout is an extra layer of complexity you have to solve. How many buttons can you fit on a screen without it feeling too busy? These questions are crucial to the design.
